## Everything around the model, solved once

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Turns, not messages" icon="comment">
    Bursts, voice notes and typing become one `turn.ready`. A new message supersedes the turn, so your agent answers everything at once.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Texts like a human" icon="pencil">
    Read receipts, "typing…" sized to the words, replies in natural parts with jittered pauses. It stops when the person starts writing again.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Humans always win" icon="approve-check">
    Answer from your phone and the agent steps back on its own, then returns after a quiet spell.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Never loses a reply" icon="list-format">
    A durable outbox survives restarts, retries with the same WhatsApp id, keeps per-chat order and says why anything waits.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hears and sees" icon="magnifier">
    Voice notes and photos arrive as text through Whisper, any vision model, your own service or a local command.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keeps the number safe" icon="warning">
    Limits, quiet hours, cold-outreach block, a kill switch and early warnings when people start blocking you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rehearse before you ship" icon="star">
    Shadow mode on live traffic, replays of real days at 100x, and scripted conversations for your agent's CI.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Featherweight" icon="rocket">
    One native Go process, about 21 MB of RAM, full history in SQLite with full-text search.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## One event in, one call out

```http
POST /api/default/turns/t_3f1c…/reply
{"parts": [{"text": "Hi Ann!"}, {"text": "Yes, card or cash on delivery."}]}
```

Timing, typing, read receipts, retries, ordering, takeover and limits are WaGo's job. [See a whole turn](/wago/getting-started/#one-turn-start-to-finish).

## Built for agents to drive

The [For AI agents](/wago/for-agents/) page is written for models, and [`llms.txt`](/wago/llms.txt) indexes the docs. Hand your coding agent this:

```text
Set up WaGo (https://github.com/nik1t7n/wago) for me. Read its AGENTS.md first and follow its rules.
Run it with -dev-fake, connect my agent's webhook to turn.ready, and prove the loop with /dev/incoming.
Then show me how to link my phone. Do not turn on sending or message real people without asking me.
```
